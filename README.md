# Chaotic Good Barbarian

## Chaotic Good Barbarian

Medium humanoid, chaotic good. Challenge 27 (105,000 XP), Proficiency Bonus +8.

Chaotic Good Barbarian was cast out of his tribe for refusing to raid a defenseless village. Now he wanders the roads breaking up what he calls "bully work": slavers, corrupt tax collectors, anyone picking on people smaller than them.

**The code:** never hit down, never leave a friend behind, and if a tyrant needs smashing, smash first and ask questions never.

**Stats:** STR 30 (+10), DEX 23 (+6), CON 30 (+10), INT 16 (+3), WIS 20 (+5), CHA 25 (+7). HP 580 (40d8 + 400), AC 26 (Unarmored Defense), Speed 40 ft., climb 40 ft., swim 40 ft. Challenge 27 (105,000 XP), Proficiency Bonus +8. Saving Throws STR +18, DEX +14, CON +18, WIS +13. Skills Acrobatics +14, Athletics +18, Animal Handling +13, Insight +13, Intimidation +15, Performance +15, Persuasion +15, Perception +13, Survival +13. Damage Resistances Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing. Damage Immunities Poison. Condition Immunities Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone (unless he wishes to be). Senses Passive Perception 23. Languages Common, Dwarvish, Draconic, Undercommon.

**Signature moves:** Legendary Resistance (3/Day), Juicy Muscles (3/Round), Absolute Unit, Siege Monster, Menacing Presence (1/Round), Levels in Bard (Barbaric Inspiration), Danger Sense, Feral Instinct, Reckless, Brutal Critical, Relentless Rage, Playthings.

**Gear:** greataxe, 2 handaxes, explorer's pack, and a dented tankard he refuses to throw away.
