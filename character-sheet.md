# Chaotic Good Barbarian — Character Sheet

Medium humanoid, chaotic good

- **Armor Class:** 26 (Unarmored Defense)
- **Hit Points:** 580 (40d8 + 400)
- **Speed:** 40 ft., climb 40 ft., swim 40 ft.
- **Challenge:** 27 (105,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +8

## Ability Scores

- STR 30 (+10)
- DEX 23 (+6)
- CON 30 (+10)
- INT 16 (+3)
- WIS 20 (+5)
- CHA 25 (+7)

## Saving Throws

STR +18, DEX +14, CON +18, WIS +13

## Skills

Acrobatics +14, Athletics +18, Animal Handling +13, Insight +13, Intimidation +15, Performance +15, Persuasion +15, Perception +13, Survival +13

## Damage Resistances

Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing

## Damage Immunities

Poison

## Condition Immunities

Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone (unless he wishes to be)

## Senses

Passive Perception 23

## Languages

Common, Dwarvish, Draconic, Undercommon

## Features

- **Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If he fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
- **Juicy Muscles (3/Round):** When he takes damage, subtract his Constitution modifier from the total.
- **Absolute Unit:** He can shove and grapple creatures up to two sizes larger than him, and counts as two sizes larger for carrying capacity.
- **Siege Monster:** He deals double damage to objects and structures.
- **Menacing Presence (1/Round):** He can force a creature within 60 feet to succeed on a DC 23 Wisdom saving throw or become Frightened.
- **Levels in Bard:** He has advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks, and can use Barbaric Inspiration as a bonus action.
- **Danger Sense:** He has advantage on Dexterity saving throws while not Blinded, Deafened, or Incapacitated.
- **Feral Instinct:** He has advantage on initiative rolls.
- **Reckless:** He has advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during his turn.
- **Brutal Critical:** He scores a critical hit on a 19-20, and deals an extra 3d10 damage on critical hits.
- **Relentless Rage:** If he falls to 0 hit points while raging and isn't killed outright, he can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw to drop to 1 hit point instead.
- **Playthings:** He can use any object or prone creature of Medium size or smaller within 5 feet as an improvised weapon.

## Backstory

Chaotic Good Barbarian grew up in a mountain tribe that paid its way by raiding. When the chief ordered a raid on a farming village that had nothing worth taking, Chaotic Good Barbarian refused, broke the chief's jaw, and walked out. He has been walking ever since, hiring out his axe to anyone who needs a wall between them and something bigger.

Chaotic Good Barbarian was cast out of his tribe for refusing to raid a defenseless village. Now he wanders the roads breaking up what he calls "bully work": slavers, corrupt tax collectors, anyone picking on people smaller than them.
