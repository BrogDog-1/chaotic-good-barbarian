# Chaotic Good Barbarian — Character Sheet

- **Race:** Half-orc
- **Class:** Barbarian 3 (Path of the Totem Warrior — Bear)
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Background:** Outlander (exiled tribesman)
- **Level:** 3

## Ability Scores

- STR 16 (+3)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 16 (+3)
- INT 8 (-1)
- WIS 12 (+1)
- CHA 10 (+0)

- **HP:** 35 (3d12 + 9)
- **AC:** 15 (10 + DEX + CON, Unarmored Defense)
- **Speed:** 30 ft
- **Proficiency bonus:** +2

## Saving Throws

- STR +5, CON +5 (proficient)
- DEX +2, INT -1, WIS +1, CHA +0

## Skills

- Athletics +5, Intimidation +2, Perception +3, Survival +3, Animal Handling +3

## Features

- **Rage (3/day):** +2 damage, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing. Bear totem adds resistance to all damage except psychic while raging.
- **Reckless Attack:** advantage on Strength attacks; attacks against you have advantage until your next turn.
- **Danger Sense:** advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see.
- **Unarmored Defense:** AC equals 10 + Dexterity + Constitution.
- **Spirit Seeker:** can cast beast sense and speak with animals as rituals.
- **Relentless Endurance (half-orc):** when reduced to 0 HP, drop to 1 HP instead, once per long rest.
- **Savage Attacks (half-orc):** one extra damage die on a critical hit.
- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.

## Equipment

- Greataxe (1d12 + 3 slashing)
- 2 handaxes (1d6 + 3)
- 4 javelins
- Explorer's pack
- Tribal totem necklace, dented tankard, 15 gp

## Backstory

Chaotic Good Barbarian grew up in a mountain tribe that paid its way by raiding. When the chief ordered a raid on a farming village that had nothing worth taking, Chaotic Good Barbarian refused, broke the chief's jaw, and walked out. He has been walking ever since, hiring out his axe to anyone who needs a wall between them and something bigger.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Laughs loud, hits harder, cries at campfire songs.
- **Ideal:** Freedom — nobody gets to own another person.
- **Bond:** The village he refused to raid. He checks on it when he can.
- **Flaw:** Zero patience for rules, tolls, or anyone who says "that's just how it is."
